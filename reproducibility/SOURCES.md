# Primary sources

Retrieved and checked on 6 August 2026.

## 1. Exact bounty statement

Eric Allender, *Parting Thoughts and Parting Shots (Read On for Details on How to Win Valuable Prizes!)*, SIGACT News 54(1), 2023.

Official author PDF:

https://people.cs.rutgers.edu/~allender/papers/sigact.news.draft.pdf

Relevant location: Section 4, “ACC0 Again: A Retraction”, Open Question 3. The question asks whether every language accepted by constant-width circuit families of polylogarithmic genus lies in `ACC^0`, with a $1000 reward.

## 2. Retracted earlier proof and current correction

Eric Allender, Samir Datta, Sambuddha Roy, *Topology inside NC^1*, ECCC TR04-108 / CCC 2005.

ECCC record:

https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/eccc-reports/2004/TR04-108/index.html

The ECCC record includes Eric Allender's 2 November 2025 comment stating that the proof of Theorem 6 is incorrect and that the theorem was not known from that paper.

## 3. Planar constant-width characterization

Kristoffer Arnsfelt Hansen, *Constant width planar computation characterizes ACC0*, ECCC TR03-025; later published in *Theory of Computing Systems* 39(1), 2006.

ECCC record:

https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/eccc-reports/2003/TR03-025/index.html

This is the external complexity-theoretic theorem used for planar macroblocks.

## 4. Additivity of orientable graph genus

Joseph Battle, Frank Harary, Yukihiro Kodama, J. W. T. Youngs, *Additivity of the genus of a graph*, Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society 68 (1962), 565-568.

DOI:

https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9904-1962-10847-7

This is the classical topological result used to bound the number of nonplanar connected components by the total orientable genus.

## Source discipline

The package links to external papers rather than copying them. A reviewer should check the exact theorem statements in the originals, especially the circuit basis, width convention, polynomial-size requirement, and genus convention.
